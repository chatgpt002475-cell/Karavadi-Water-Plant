## 2. ENGINEERING FORMULAS & PROCESS DERIVATIONS

### 2.1 Membrane Permeate Flux Formulation
Water flux ($J_w$) through a reverse osmosis semi-permeable membrane is given by:
$$J_w = A \cdot (\Delta P - \Delta \pi)$$
Where:
- $J_w$ = Pure water permeate flux ($\text{L/m}^2\cdot\text{hr}$ or $\text{LMH}$)
- $A$ = Membrane water permeability coefficient ($\approx 1.45\text{ LMH/bar}$ at $25^\circ\text{C}$)
- $\Delta P$ = Net hydraulic transmembrane pressure differential:
  $$\Delta P = \frac{P_{feed} + P_{concentrate}}{2} - P_{permeate}$$
  For $P_{feed} = 14.5\text{ bar}$, $P_{concentrate} = 13.5\text{ bar}$, $P_{permeate} = 0.5\text{ bar}$:
  $$\Delta P = \frac{14.5 + 13.5}{2} - 0.5 = 14.0 - 0.5 = 13.5\text{ bar}$$
- $\Delta \pi$ = Osmotic pressure differential across the active membrane skin.

---

### 2.2 Osmotic Pressure Derivation by van 't Hoff Equation
$$\pi = \sum (i \cdot M_j \cdot R \cdot T)$$
For brackish groundwater dominated by $NaCl$ and $CaSO_4$ salts, osmotic pressure approximates:
$$\pi \approx 0.00076 \cdot TDS\text{ (mg/L)}\text{ in bar}$$
At average membrane surface TDS ($TDS_{avg} \approx 1,700\text{ mg/L}$):
$$\pi_{feed/brine} = 0.00076 \times 1,700 = 1.29\text{ bar}$$
$$\pi_{permeate} = 0.00076 \times 35 = 0.03\text{ bar}$$
$$\Delta \pi = 1.29 - 0.03 = 1.26\text{ bar}$$

**Resulting Water Flux:**
$$J_w = 1.45 \cdot (13.5 - 1.26) = 1.45 \times 12.24 = 17.75\text{ LMH}$$

**Required Active Membrane Area ($A_m$):**
$$A_m = \frac{Q_p}{J_w} = \frac{2,000\text{ LPH}}{17.75\text{ LMH}} = 112.67\text{ m}^2$$
> Sizing Verification: 1 unit of standard **8040 spiral wound membrane** has an active surface area of **$37.2\text{ m}^2$** ($400\text{ ft}^2$).  
> With 4 elements: $Total\text{ Area} = 4 \times 37.2 = 148.8\text{ m}^2$.  
> Margin of Safety: $\frac{148.8 - 112.67}{112.67} \times 100 = \mathbf{+32.0\%}$ safety buffer against long-term membrane compaction and aging.

---

### 2.3 Specific Energy Consumption (SEC) of RO System
$$SEC = \frac{P_{electrical}}{Q_{permeate}}$$
Where:
- High Pressure Pump = $4.0\text{ kW}$
- Raw Feed & Dosing Pumps = $1.8\text{ kW}$
- Total Continuous Electrical Load = $5.8\text{ kW}$
- Permeate Production Rate = $2.0\text{ m}^3/\text{hr}$
$$SEC = \frac{5.8\text{ kW}}{2.0\text{ m}^3/\text{hr}} = 2.90\text{ kWh/m}^3\text{ of pure water}$$
$$\text{Electricity Cost per Litre (at ₹7.80/kWh)} = \frac{2.90 \times ₹7.80}{1,000\text{ L}} = \mathbf{₹0.0226\text{ per Litre (₹0.45 per 20L Can)}}$$

---

### 2.4 Ozone Disinfection Contact Math ($C \cdot t$ Calculation)
Under BIS IS 14543, packaged water must maintain a residual ozone concentration of **0.20 to 0.40 mg/L** at the time of bottling.
$$C \cdot t = \text{Concentration (mg/L)} \times \text{Contact Time (minutes)}$$
For pathogen inactivation (*Giardia, Cryptosporidium*, Viruses, and *Coliforms*):
- Required $C \cdot t$ for $99.99\%$ (4-log) viral kill = $1.2\text{ mg}\cdot\text{min/L}$
- With $C = 0.30\text{ mg/L}$ ozone in water:
$$t_{contact} = \frac{1.2}{0.30} = 4.0\text{ minutes}$$
- Storage volume required in SS304 contact tank at $2,000\text{ LPH}$ ($33.3\text{ L/min}$):
$$V_{contact} = 33.3\text{ L/min} \times 4.0\text{ min} = 133.2\text{ Litres}$$
Our installed buffer tank ($5,000\text{ Litres}$) provides over **150 minutes of residence time**, guaranteeing absolute micro sterility.

## 3. INSTRUMENT CALIBRATION PROTOCOL (STEP-BY-STEP SOP)

```mermaid
graph TD
    A[Step 1: Physical Inspection & Zero-Check] --> B[Step 2: Buffer Standard Verification (1413 µS/cm or pH 4.0/7.0)]
    B --> C{Within ±2% Tolerance?}
    C -- Yes --> D[Step 3: Affix Signed Calibration Sticker & Log Date]
    C -- No --> E[Step 4: Gain/Slope Potentiometer Trim or Sensor Cleaning]
    E --> B
```

### Calibration Procedure for On-Line Conductivity / TDS Cell:
1. Isolate the sensor bypass line using SS needle valves.
2. Remove electrode and rinse thoroughly with $100\text{ ml}$ HPLC-grade deionized water ($<1\text{ \mu S/cm}$).
3. Immerse in certified NABL traceable Standard Potassium Chloride ($KCl$) Solution ($1413\text{ \mu S/cm}$ at $25.0^\circ\text{C}$).
4. Allow 90 seconds for temperature sensor thermistor equilibrium.
5. If LCD display deviates outside $1385 - 1441\text{ \mu S/cm}$ ($\pm 2\%$), access calibration mode and enter offset calibration factor.
6. Re-immerse in secondary check standard ($147\text{ \mu S/cm}$); verify linearity.
7. Record in Instrument Logbook Form **QC-CAL-04** with signoff of approved Quality Chemist.
