## PHASE 1: GROUND WATER TESTING, HYDROGEOLOGY & SOURCE SECURITY

```
[Borewell Drilling & Yield Test] ──> [NABL Comprehensive Lab Test] ──> [Treatment Matrix Design] ──> [APWALTA/CGWA NOC]
```

### 1.1 Borewell Siting & Yield Capacity
To sustainably operate a **2,000 LPH RO plant**, your source must supply **4,000 to 5,000 LPH of raw water**, because standard commercial RO systems produce **40% to 50% permeate (pure water)** and **50% to 60% reject (brine water)** when handling typical Prakasam district ground water TDS levels (700–1,800 ppm).

- **Minimum Required Yield:** 1.0 to 1.2 Lakh Litres per 24 hours (approx. 20–25 Gallons Per Minute / 4,500–5,500 LPH continuous).
- **Depth Benchmarks:** In the Karavadi/Gundlakamma river belt, good aquifers sit between **180 ft and 350 ft**. Avoid shallow borewells (<100 ft) due to agricultural pesticide runoff and seasonal summer depletion.
- **Yield Verification Test:** Run a continuous **8-hour compressor/pump drawdown test** in peak summer (March–May) to confirm aquifer recharge rates before building civil structures.
- **Dual Borewell Strategy:** Always drill **two borewells** (Primary + Standby spaced at least 50 meters apart) with automatic alternate starter controllers. If one pump burns out or silts up, production never stops.

---

### 1.2 NABL Laboratory Water Testing Protocol
Before purchasing machinery, take **5 Litres of raw borewell water** in clean, sterilized food-grade containers to an accredited NABL testing laboratory (e.g., King Institute, SGS, TUV, or AP State Water Testing Labs in Vijayawada/Guntur).

#### Critical Water Quality Parameters to Test:
| Parameter | Acceptable Raw Range for 2000 LPH RO | Treatment Mechanism in Plant | BIS IS 14543 Finished Limit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **pH** | 6.5 – 8.5 | Normalization / Dosing | 6.5 – 8.5 |
| **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)** | 500 – 2,000 mg/L | High Pressure RO Membrane (4040/8040) | 75 – 500 mg/L (Sweet spot: 110–140 mg/L) |
| **Total Hardness ($CaCO_3$)** | 200 – 600 mg/L | Antiscalant Dosing + RO Permeation | Max 200 mg/L |
| **Chlorides ($Cl^-$)** | 150 – 800 mg/L | RO Desalination Membranes | Max 200 mg/L |
| **Sulphates ($SO_4^{2-}$)** | 100 – 400 mg/L | RO Membranes | Max 200 mg/L |
| **Iron ($Fe$)** | < 0.3 mg/L (If >0.3, triggers scaling) | DMI-65 / Manganese Greensand Filter | Max 0.1 mg/L |
| **Silica ($SiO_2$)** | < 25 mg/L | Specialist Antiscalant (Phosphonate) | Max trace |
| **Turbidity** | < 10 NTU | Multi-Grade Sand Filter (MGF) | Max 2 NTU |
| **Total Coliform / E. coli** | Can be present in borewell | Pre-Chlorination + Ozonation + UV | **0 CFU / 250 ml (Must be ABSENT)** |

> ⚠️ **Prakasam District Caution:** Groundwater around Ongole and coastal AP often exhibits moderate to high salinity (chlorides) and seasonal hardness spikes. Never size an RO system assuming city municipal tap water. Insist on brackish water (BW) membranes (e.g., Dow Filmtec BW30-4040 or Hydranautics CPA2).

## PHASE 5: DAILY PRODUCTION STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (SOPS) & LAB QC

### 5.1 Morning Startup Routine (30-Minute Protocol)
1. **Raw Sump Verification:** Inspect raw sump level ($>10,000\text{ Litres}$). Check chlorine dosing tank has fresh 1% sodium hypochlorite solution.
2. **Filter Backwashing:**
   - Backwash Sand Filter for 10 minutes until wastewater runs crystal clear.
   - Backwash Activated Carbon Filter for 10 minutes; rinse for 5 minutes.
3. **Antiscalant Dosing Tank:** Verify antiscalant chemical (dosing rate: 3–5 ppm / $30\text{ ml}$ per 1,000L). Never run RO pump dry without antiscalant.
4. **Membrane Startup & Flush:**
   - Open reject valve fully.
   - Start high-pressure pump. Flush membranes for 2 minutes to discharge stagnant water.
   - Adjust high-pressure needle valve to achieve 2,000 LPH permeate flow and 2,000–2,500 LPH reject flow.
   - Verify membrane feed pressure: **140 to 180 PSI**. (If pressure exceeds 220 PSI, chemical cleaning/CIP is urgently required).
5. **Ozonator Calibration:** Ensure ozone generator runs at optimal oxygen flow. Measure residual ozone in treated tank: **Target: 0.2 to 0.4 ppm**.

### 5.2 20-Litre Jar Washing & Sanitization Protocol
Most customer complaints in the 20L can business stem from dirty cans, not the RO water. Execute this **4-Stage Sanitization SOP**:

```
Step 1: External Scrubbing (Removes outside mud, grime, grease using high-pressure reject water jet)
   │
Step 2: Internal De-greasing & Disinfection (Hot water 50°C wash with 2% Food Grade Caustic / Peracetic Acid)
   │
Step 3: Sterile Water Rinsing (20-second rinse using 0.5 ppm Ozonated RO water)
   │
Step 4: Automatic Gravity Filling & Instant Pneumatic Capping (Within Class 100 sterile enclosure)
   │
Step 5: Tamper-Evident Heat Shrink Neck Seal (Heat gun application of printed branded shrink band)
```

## PHASE 8: OPERATIONS CONTROL, ASSET SECURITY & CASHFLOW PROTECTION

### 8.1 Preventing Common Fatal Traps in Water Bottling
1. **Membrane Scaling Death:**
   - Hardness salts precipitate and permanently blind RO membranes within 90 days if antiscalant runs dry.
   - *Control:* Connect the antiscalant dosing pump electrical interlock directly to the high-pressure pump starter. If the dosing pump is off, the RO pump cannot start.
2. **Driver & Sales Cash Leakage:**
   - Drivers collecting daily cash for 20L cans often report short collections or bad debts.
   - *Control:* Implement **Digital UPI QR Codes** directly on the can's neck seal. Customers scan and pay directly to the company bank account.
   - Drivers must tally: $\text{Loaded Jars} = \text{Full Jars Returned} + \text{Empty Jars Returned} + \text{Cash/UPI Receipts}$.
3. **Credit (Khata) Traps:**
   - Never give more than 1 delivery cycle of credit to any restaurant or shop.
   - If they have not paid for Monday's cans, do not drop off Wednesday's delivery. In this business, uncollected receivables wipe out months of profit.

### A.2 Scheduled Maintenance Calender
- **Daily:** Backwash Sand & Carbon Filters (10 mins each). Check antiscalant level.
- **Weekly:** Clean 5µ and 1µ micron filter cartridges. Replace if delta pressure $>1.0\text{ Bar}$. Check UV lamp radiation status.
- **Monthly:** Full CIP (Clean-In-Place) chemical washing of RO membranes using 1% citric acid (inorganic scales) followed by 0.1% sodium hydroxide (organic foulants).
- **Quarterly:** Calibrate all digital pH, TDS, and pressure gauges against lab buffer standards.
- **Yearly:** Replace 100% of Activated Carbon media (Coconut shell granular carbon loses adsorption capacity after 12–14 months).
