## 4. CIVIL ENGINEERING CALCULATIONS & HVAC FORMULAS

### 4.1 Underground Raw Water Sump Sizing
To cushion an 8-hour continuous RO run ($4,444\text{ LPH raw water}$) during power failure or borewell maintenance:
$$V_{sump} = Q_{in} \cdot t_{buffer}$$
$$V_{sump} = 4,444\text{ LPH} \times 5.0\text{ hours} = 22,220\text{ Litres} \approx 25.0\text{ m}^3$$

**Civil Dimensions for $25\text{ m}^3$ Rectangular RCC Sump:**
- Length ($L$) = $4.5\text{ m}$
- Width ($W$) = $3.0\text{ m}$
- Effective Depth ($D$) = $1.9\text{ m}$ ($+0.4\text{ m}$ freeboard $= 2.3\text{ m}$ total height)
- Total Storage $= 4.5 \times 3.0 \times 1.9 = 25.65\text{ m}^3 = 25,650\text{ Litres}$

---

### 4.2 Cleanroom Air Exchanges (ACH) & HVAC Airflow (CFM) Formula
The Class 100/1000 filling room must maintain an ISO 7 / ISO 8 background with localized ISO 5 at the filling nozzle.
$$CFM = \frac{V_{room} \times ACH}{60}$$
Where:
- Room Length ($L$) = $10.0\text{ m} = 32.8\text{ ft}$
- Room Width ($W$) = $6.5\text{ m} = 21.3\text{ ft}$
- Room Ceiling Height ($H$) = $3.2\text{ m} = 10.5\text{ ft}$
- $V_{room} = 32.8 \times 21.3 \times 10.5 = 7,336\text{ ft}^3$
- Required Air Changes per Hour ($ACH$) = **30 exchanges/hour** for Class 1000
$$CFM = \frac{7,336 \times 30}{60} = 3,668\text{ CFM}$$
> Sizing: Install a **4,000 CFM Air Handling Unit (AHU)** with DX cooling coil ($7.5\text{ TR}$ capacity, maintaining $22^\circ\text{C} \pm 2^\circ\text{C}$ and $50\% \pm 5\%$ Relative Humidity) passing through EU-4 pre-filters, EU-9 intermediate micro-filters, and terminal **H14 HEPA filters (99.997% efficiency at 0.3 micron)**.

---

### 4.3 Positive Pressure Cascade Equation
Air must always flow outward from the ultra-clean zone to the warehouse:
$$\Delta P = P_{Filling} - P_{Corridor} \ge 15\text{ Pascals}$$
$$\Delta P = P_{Corridor} - P_{Warehouse} \ge 10\text{ Pascals}$$
The required air leakage through cracks/door gaps ($Q_L$) to sustain this pressure is given by:
$$Q_L = C_d \cdot A_{crack} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{2 \cdot \Delta P}{\rho_{air}}}$$
Where discharge coefficient $C_d \approx 0.65$ and air density $\rho_{air} = 1.20\text{ kg/m}^3$.

---

### 4.4 Sanitary Drainage Slope & Manning's Velocity
Floor gutters in the bottle washing and RO area must prevent standing puddles (which breed *Pseudomonas* bacteria).
$$v = \frac{1}{n} \cdot R^{2/3} \cdot S^{1/2}$$
Where:
- Manning's roughness coefficient $n = 0.011$ (Smooth SS304 half-round trench)
- Hydraulic radius $R = \frac{A}{P_w} = \frac{0.015\text{ m}^2}{0.35\text{ m}} = 0.0428\text{ m}$
- Minimum design floor slope $S = 1:50 = 0.02$
$$v = \frac{1}{0.011} \cdot (0.0428)^{2/3} \cdot (0.02)^{1/2} = 90.91 \cdot 0.1224 \cdot 0.1414 = 1.57\text{ m/s}$$
> An outflow velocity of **$1.57\text{ m/s}$** ensures continuous self-scouring and zero bacterial biofilm stagnant zones.
