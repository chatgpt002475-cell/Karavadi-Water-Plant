- **1L PET Case of 12 Bottles:** Manufacturing Cost: **₹50.16** | Sold to Dealer: **₹72.00** | Net Profit: **₹21.84 (30.3% margin)**.
- **Retailer Pitch:** Retail shop sells 1L case at **₹240 MRP** (buys at ₹100 from dealer), yielding **₹140 profit per case** (vs only ₹80-₹90 on Bisleri/Kinley).
