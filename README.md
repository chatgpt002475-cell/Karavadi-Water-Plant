# Karavadi Water Plant (KPW)

Comprehensive repository containing managerial, technical, financial, and operational documentation for the Karavadi Water Plant.

---

## Repository Structure

### 📁 Managerial Documents (`KPW_MANAGERIAL_Documents/`)
- **M1_Payroll_Register.xlsx**: Payroll records and staff compensation tracking.
- **M2_Financial_Dashboard.xlsx**: Core financial summaries and metrics dashboard.
- **M3_KPI_Scorecard.xlsx**: Operational and business KPI evaluation scorecard.
- **M4_Budget_vs_Actual.xlsx**: Financial budget variances and actual expenditure comparisons.
- **M5_Risk_Register.xlsx**: Operational, environmental, and compliance risk assessments.
- **M6_Business_Plan.docx**: Comprehensive business plan for operations.
- **M7_HR_Policy_Manual.docx**: Human resources governance, employment terms, and company policies.
- **M8_Marketing_Strategy.pptx**: Brand positioning, marketing channels, and outreach plan.
- **M9_Investor_Pitch_Deck.pptx**: Executive presentation for investors and stakeholders.
- **M10_Expansion_Roadmap.pptx**: Strategic growth roadmap and multi-phase capacity expansion plan.
- **M11_Customer_Revenue_Analysis.xlsx**: Customer segmentation, sales volume, and revenue analytics.

---

### 📄 Technical Specifications & Budgets
- **`Karavadi_Water_Plant_Technical_Specification.docx`**: Engineering specifications, filtration details, and machinery requirements.
- **`Packaged_Drinking_Water_Plant_2000_LPH.docx`**: Technical guidelines for the 2000 LPH RO water packaging plant.
- **`Used_RO_Water_Plant_South_India_Guide.docx`**: Procurement and setup guide for RO water plants in South India.
- **`Karavadi_Capital_Budget_5L_Complete.xlsx`**: Detailed capital expenditure and budget model (5L model).
- **`Karavadi_Capital_Budget_5L.xlsx`**: Capital expenditure forecast.
- **`Karavadi_Capital_Budget.xlsx`**: Capital budget model.

---

### 📈 Marketing, Leads & Prospect Data
- **`Karavadi_40_Premium_Taglines.docx`**: Branding and premium tagline concepts for packaged water.
- **`water_leads_sales_tracker.xlsx`**: Lead pipeline and distributor sales tracking register.
- **`AP_Water_Prospects_FULL_6960.xlsx`**: Comprehensive market prospect database across Andhra Pradesh.
- **`AP_Water_Supplier_Prospects_30perMandal.xlsx`**: Mandal-wise distributor and supplier prospects.
- **`AP_Water_Supplier_Prospects.xlsx`**: Supplier and vendor prospect directory.
